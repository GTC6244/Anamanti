What it does

An assistant that lives in the room, not the cloud.

Wake it with your voice, then just talk. Anamanti listens on-device, streams to your home server for the heavy thinking, and speaks the answer back — all on the local network. Three things set it apart.

Memory

It remembers you, not just this sentence.

  • Holds onto facts and preferences across every conversation, for days and weeks
  • Recall powered by an on-device knowledge graph, scoped per person
  • Just say “remember…” or “forget that” — you stay in control
  • Memory lives on your home server: inspect it, edit it, or delete it anytime

Tools

It doesn't just answer — it acts.

  • Weather, web search, calendar, directions and recipes, on demand
  • Manages your household shopping list, timers and reminders
  • Controls music and Spotify playback around the home
  • Runs on your own accounts — and the toolkit is extensible as you add more

Communication

A real back-and-forth, not one-shot commands.

  • Natural multi-turn voice — follow-ups need no repeated wake word
  • Answers appear live on screen and are spoken aloud as they form
  • Barge in any time to interrupt, correct, or change direction
  • Proactive nudges: it reaches out first when something matters

Custom tools

It doesn't just answer. It does.

Every tool runs through your orchestrator, so actions happen on your own accounts and your own network — extensible with more as you add them.

How it works

Wake. Stream. Think. Speak.

A tablet or custom smart speaker is the ears, eyes, and voice in the room — we like repurposed Amazon Show 8 devices; your home server is the brain. They find each other automatically over your LAN.

1

Wake word

Detected fully offline on the device — no audio leaves until you summon it.

2

Stream

Your speech streams to the home server; a Whisper server transcribes it live.

3

Think

The LLM reasons with memory & tools, streaming its reply token-by-token.

4

Speak

Piper voices each sentence as it forms and plays it back on the display.

Private by design

Your home stays your home.

Anamanti was built the opposite way to a big-tech smart speaker. There's no vendor account, no always-open mic to the cloud, and no mystery about where your voice goes.

  • Wake word runs on-device — audio only leaves after you summon it
  • Speech, memory, and tools run on your own home server on the local network
  • Choose a fully-local LLM for a 100% offline brain
  • Memory is a file on your home server — inspect it, edit it, delete it
  • Auto-discovery over mDNS — no hardcoded servers, no accounts
