What it does

An assistant that lives in the room, not the cloud.

Wake it with your voice, then just talk. Anamanti listens on-device, streams to your own Mac for the heavy thinking, and speaks the answer back — all on the local network.

🗣️

Multi-turn voice control

Hold a natural conversation. After each reply Anamanti keeps listening for a beat, so follow-ups need no repeated wake word — it remembers the thread of the chat.

🔧

Custom tools

Beyond chat, it acts: check the weather, search the web, read your calendar, get directions, pull up recipes, add to the shopping list, set timers, and play music.

📺

Live on the display

Your words appear as you speak; the reply streams in token-by-token and is spoken aloud sentence-by-sentence — talking starts before the full answer is even written.

🧠

Remembers you

Persistent memory across sessions — say “remember…” or just mention a preference. Recall is powered by an on-Mac knowledge graph, scoped per person by speaker ID.

✋

Barge-in, any time

Cut it off mid-sentence by saying the wake word again. Playback stops instantly and a fresh turn begins — no waiting for it to finish.

🔔

Proactive notifications

Anamanti can reach out first — a reminder, an alert — pushed straight to the display over a connection your home holds open. It speaks when spoken to, and nudges when it matters.

Custom tools

It doesn't just answer. It does.

Every tool runs through your orchestrator, so actions happen on your own accounts and your own network — extensible with more as you add them.

How it works

Wake. Stream. Think. Speak.

A jailbroken Echo Show 8 is the ears, eyes, and voice in the room; an M4 Mac Mini is the brain. They find each other automatically over your LAN.

1

Wake word

Detected fully offline on the device — no audio leaves until you summon it.

2

Stream

Your speech streams to the Mac; a Whisper server transcribes it live.

3

Think

The LLM reasons with memory & tools, streaming its reply token-by-token.

4

Speak

Piper voices each sentence as it forms and plays it back on the display.

Private by design

Your home stays your home.

Anamanti was built the opposite way to a big-tech smart speaker. There's no vendor account, no always-open mic to the cloud, and no mystery about where your voice goes.

  • Wake word runs on-device — audio only leaves after you summon it
  • Speech, memory, and tools run on your own Mac on the local network
  • Choose a fully-local LLM for a 100% offline brain
  • Memory is a file on your machine — inspect it, edit it, delete it
  • Auto-discovery over mDNS — no hardcoded servers, no accounts
