Canonical context the assistant uses to ground answers: where "here" is (for weather and nearby questions) and who lives in the home, with their emails and phone numbers. Changes apply live, no restart.

Home

The home address, grounding "here" for weather and nearby questions. Driving directions use the full address; weather and the assistant's general "here" use only City + State/Prov. Leave fields blank if unknown.

People

Who lives in the home. Fix spellings, emails, numbers, and AppSaid addresses here — the assistant uses this roster to recognize people, answer "who" questions, and to decide who send_phone_message can text. A person needs an AppSaid address to receive phone messages (paste it, or link it from the device: Settings → Link a phone).
